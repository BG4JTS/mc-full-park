### Tier 2 — 需补充依赖（前置已解决）
Carpet TIS Addition, Carpet Extra, Carpet Org Addition, Syncmatica, Inventory Profiles Next, Chat Animation, Durability Tooltip, Easy Shulker Boxes, More Mouse Tweaks, Technical Utilities, WorldEdit

- **C2ME 与 Moonrise 不兼容** — 两者都重写区块系统
- **ScalableLux 可能影响光照更新相关红石装置** — 需测试
